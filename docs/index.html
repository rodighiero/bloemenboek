Plusieurs Especes de Fleurs Agnes Block, c. 1680–1704

Plusieurs Especes de Fleurs dessinées d'apres le Naturel

An album of 196 flowers drawn from life, compiled by the collector and gardener Agnes Block (1629–1704). Artists named in it include Maria Moninckx, Pieter Holsteyn II and Octavius M. de J. Brush in colours over pencil. The spine reads Bloemen na't leven gedaen.

Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam, RP-T-1948-119. Images in the public domain. The handwritten index and the back cover have not been digitised.
